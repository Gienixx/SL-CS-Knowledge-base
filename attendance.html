Clock-in and overtime guidance

Clock-in is available before a published or changed shift, and is also available for rest days, holidays, and dates without a released shift. Rest-day work is recorded as RDOT; no-shift work is also recorded as RDOT; holiday work is recorded as overtime. All overtime is limited to 20 hours in total per scheduled work date or no-shift work date.
